# Çizimler

Üç özgün çalışma yerleşik ImageGen ile üretildi. Balık ve dekor atlasları kırpıldı ve saydam WebP dosyalarına dönüştürüldü. Arka planın kenarları referans oranını korur. Ortadaki açık su, farklı yatay ekran oranlarını doldurur.

## Balık atlası

> Production sprite atlas for original 2D indie game BlupLab. Landscape image, 5 columns by 2 rows, exactly ten isolated independent right-facing little fish silhouettes, each centered in its own equal size grid cell with generous completely transparent clear gutters. Row 1 is round soft golden yellow puffer, turquoise fancy guppy with giant fan tail, dreamy light denim blue oval fish with hooded eyes, fluffy round coral-red pink cheek fish, slim long soft orange tiger loach with 3 amber stripes. Row 2 is perky leaf-green fish with forked tail, pale cream butterflyfish with two navy stripes and tall triangular fins, chunky muted navy blue fish with tiny whiskers, expressive pink-coral fish with flowing petal fins, tiny distinctive golden angular fish with a big fin. Strictly in profile, adorable expressive single visible eyes, varied thoughtful silhouettes, wobbly soft dark navy ink outlines, solid flat rich warm fills, subtle dry pastel print edges, analog children’s storybook. Actual transparent background.

Sırasıyla Ponpon, Yelpaze, Uykucu, Mercan, Çizgili, Filiz, Kelebek, Bıyık, Gülkuyruk ve Altınkanat. Dosyalar `public/art/fish-0.webp` ile `public/art/fish-9.webp` arasında.

Teal seaweed, orange coral, indigo stones and a tiny shell at the bottom left. Turquoise seagrass, coral pink fan coral and periwinkle pebbles at the bottom right. Center 70% is open empty blue swimming space. No fish, no house, no chest, no UI or words. Palette #8ACFF8 #006199 #315B8C #1DCED8 #F2765E #F5EBDD #FFD166.

Dosyalar `public/art/west.webp`, `public/art/water.webp`, `public/art/east.webp`.

## Dekor atlası

> Production sprite atlas for aquarium decor, 5 columns by 2 rows exactly ten isolated props centered in equal size cells on actual transparent background with clear gutters. Tall jade flowing ribbon kelp, lime green fan-leaved aquatic plant, coral pink branching antler coral, red-orange fan coral, two small periwinkle pebbles. Large blue slate rock, cream peach scallop shell, miniature cream and coral domed underwater house, amber and navy pirate treasure chest, brass anchor with teal rope. Wobbly soft dark navy ink outlines, matching stroke weights, solid warm colors, analog children’s picture book feel. No shadows, baselines, text or bubbles.

Dosyalar `public/art/decor-0.webp` ile `public/art/decor-9.webp` arasında. Tarayıcı ikonları Ponpon çiziminden türetildi.

Hatch Pet’in ChatGPT evcil hayvanı biçimi bu oyunun animasyonlarına uygun değildi. Dönüş, kuyruk hareketi, kendini şişirme, takla, süzülme ve diğer tepkiler Canvas motorunda üretilir.
